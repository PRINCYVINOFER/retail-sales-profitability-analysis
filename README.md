# Retail Sales and Profitability Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data to identify sales trends, profitability performance, high-performing categories, regional performance, top-selling products, and loss-making products.

## Business Objective
The goal is to help a retail business understand:
- Which categories and regions generate the highest sales and profit
- Which products contribute the most sales
- Which products are loss-making
- How sales and profit change over time
- Where pricing, discount, inventory, and regional strategy may need improvement

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning, calculated columns, PivotTables, and charts
- Power BI: Data modeling, DAX measures, dashboard development, slicers, and visual analysis
- SQL: Data querying and business analysis

## Dataset
Sample Superstore retail dataset containing order, customer, product, category, region, sales, quantity, discount, and profit information.

## Data Cleaning
- Checked for missing values
- Verified data types for date, numeric, and text fields
- Checked duplicate records
- Created calculated columns including Year, Month, Year-Month, Profit Margin, and Shipping Days

## DAX Measures
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Overall Profit Margin
- Average Order Value

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for Sales, Profit, Profit Margin, Orders, and Customers
- Monthly Sales and Profit Trend
- Profit by Category
- Sales and Profit by Region
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Product Performance Table with conditional formatting for loss-making products
- Interactive slicers for Year, Category, and Customer Segment

## Business Recommendations
- Review products with negative profit and assess pricing or discount policies.
- Focus marketing and inventory planning on the most profitable categories and regions.
- Monitor sales and profit together to avoid prioritizing high-revenue but low-profit products.
- Investigate low-profit regions, categories, and products before making operational decisions.
